# Phase 1: Generic AI-design pattern catalogue

Rebrand mode. Use this as a detection checklist while reading the repo. For every match, record the specific file or component in `taste/AUDIT.md`. "Generic hero" is not a finding; "hero in `components/Hero.tsx` uses a 135deg purple-to-blue gradient background with a floating laptop mockup and no real product screenshot" is a finding.

For the CSS and layout layer (typography, colour, layout, shadows, spacing), invoke the `redesign-existing-projects` skill's audit section rather than re-deriving it here; it is the authoritative source for that layer. This file covers the layers that skill does not: copy and content, structure and product, motion, and brand. These are the things specific to "this was clearly generated by an AI SaaS builder."

In greenfield mode there is no repo to audit, but the same list is the Phase 5 self-check: a new build that exhibits any of these has reproduced the thing the client came to escape.

## Copy and content

- **"Powered by AI" or "AI-powered" in the hero.** Meaningless now; assume the reader already knows. State the outcome instead of the mechanism.
- **Vague benefit statements.** "Revolutionize your workflow", "Supercharge your productivity", "Unlock your potential" say nothing about what actually happens. Every headline should be falsifiable: a reader should be able to tell whether the claim is true of their situation.
- **Feature dump with no narrative.** A list of capabilities with nothing connecting them, no sense of who this is for, what they were struggling with, what changes. Features should answer a need the copy already established, never appear as an unmotivated list.
- **Generic testimonials.** Stock-photo avatars, invented names, vague praise. If there are no real customers yet, do not fake them; use a founder note, a problem statement or a live demo instead of fabricated social proof.
- **Fake "as seen in" press rows** with no coverage behind them.
- **Boilerplate FAQ** answering questions nobody asks ("Is my data safe?" answered with "Yes, we take security seriously") instead of the real objections a buyer has.
- **"Get Started" on every CTA**, undifferentiated between hero, pricing and footer. Each one should say what the visitor is actually about to do.

## Structure and product

- **Floating laptop or phone mockup in the hero**, especially isometric with a drop shadow. The single most recognisable AI-builder tell.
- **Gradient blob or mesh backgrounds** with no relationship to the product's visual language: decoration standing in for identity.
- **Particle or starfield backgrounds** (`tsparticles`, canvas dot fields) as generic "tech" ambiance rather than a deliberate choice.
- **Icon-grid feature sections** with default Lucide or Heroicons in coloured circle badges, one per feature, no hierarchy between them.
- **No real product screenshots.** If the product exists and works, the site shows it working.
- **Stock-photo people** in business casual, pointing at laptops or shaking hands.
- **Typewriter hero text** cycling through nouns. A template default, rarely load-bearing.
- **Trust badges with no substance** ("SOC 2 Compliant", "GDPR Ready") with no compliance page behind them.

## Motion

- **Zero motion anywhere.** Everything renders instantly, no transitions, no scroll response, no hover feedback. Reads as unfinished, not minimal.
- **Motion with no purpose.** Elements fade, float and bounce on scroll with no relationship to hierarchy, because a template put `motion.div` on everything. As much a tell as no motion.
- **Uniform easing everywhere.** Every transition on the same default curve, or worse, linear, so nothing differs in weight from anything else.

## Brand

- **No name story.** The product has a name and nothing explains why, or connects it to what the product does.
- **A wordmark as the logo.** The company name in a default system or Google font, often Inter Bold, sometimes with a generic gradient icon that has no conceptual link to the product. Also a standing refusal on this account; see `PREFERENCES.md`.
- **One default accent, usually blue or purple**, applied uniformly with no tonal range. The fastest way to look like every SaaS landing page of 2023 to 2025.
- **No distinct voice.** Read three sentences from the homepage aloud; if they would fit any competitor's site, there is no voice yet.
